LAB 3 · STARTER GAME

Coin Dash - Alon, Einav and Tal

Collect yellow coins. Dodge pink invaders. How many can you collect in 45 seconds?

Move: arrow keys / WASD · Restart: R · Difficulty: 1 / 2 / 3

Added features

  • Retro arcade lookPixel-art player, coins and animated invaders on a black starfield.
  • DifficultyPress 1 / 2 / 3 for Easy, Normal or Hard: fewer or more invaders, slower or faster.
  • High scoreBest score per difficulty, kept after a reload.
  • Freeze starA star appears every 10 s; grab it to freeze the invaders for 3 s.
  • Countdown"3, 2, 1, GO!" before every round; nothing moves and the 45 s timer waits until GO.
